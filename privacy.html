Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026 · Queensland, Australia

Let's Torq is a phone app for finding car and bike meets in South East Queensland. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It is written for the current Android and web preview. It is not legal advice.

Who runs this

Let's Torq is operated by Benjamin Taylor in Queensland, Australia. Contact us in the app (You). We do not use the BT Machine & Tool sales inbox for Let's Torq.

What the app does

You sign in, see meets on a map, join ones you want to go to, and can post a meet of your own. Chat and push notifications are planned and not in this first Android build.

What we collect

  • Account. Email sign-in: your email address and the display name you give us. Facebook and Instagram buttons are in the app but do not complete a Meta login yet. If we turn those on later, Meta would send us your name, email, profile photo, and a provider ID. We never see or store your Facebook or Instagram password.
  • Meets you post. Title, time, address or suburb, description, and meet type (Mixed, Bike, V8, JDM, Rotary, Race).
  • Join / RSVP. That you tapped Join on a meet, so we can show a going count and (later) that meet's chat.
  • Map. We show a map of listed meet locations. This Android build uses internet access for the map and the app. It does not request GPS or background location. If we later add on-location check-in, we will ask for location permission then and update this page.
  • Later, if we ship them: per-meet chat messages, and a device push token so we can send two kinds of notification only: a new meet nearby, and a reminder before a meet you joined. We will not use push for marketing.
  • App and device basics. Crash logs and the usual Android / Play technical data needed to run and update the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run ads in this version.
  • We do not track you around SEQ in the background.
  • We do not mix Let's Torq accounts with BT Machine & Tool or SIXb Speedshop.

Who else sees it

Meets you post and your display name on a Join count are visible to other people using Let's Torq. Hosts we use to run the app (hosting, email sign-in, and later Meta if you choose that sign-in) process data on our instructions. Google Play processes the listing and installs.

Keeping it

We keep account and meet data while your account is open and the meet is listed. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data. Posted meets that other people still need may stay listed with your name removed where we can.

Your choices

  • Don't sign in, and you cannot use Discover (the map is gated on purpose).
  • Don't tap Facebook or Instagram, and we never receive Meta data.
  • Ask in the app to delete your account. We will action that as soon as we reasonably can.

Australian privacy law applies, including the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply to us. If you have a complaint you cannot sort with us, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Children

Let's Torq is not aimed at children under 16. Do not create an account if you are under 16.

Changes

If we add chat, notifications, Meta sign-in, or location check-in, we will update this page before those features go live.
